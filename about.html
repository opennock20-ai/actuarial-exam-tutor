About me

I’m Oliver.

I’m a qualified actuary and actuarial manager, and I tutor CM1, CS1 and CB1 alongside my actuarial career.

Oliver

Why I started tutoring

I have been through the IFoA exam process relatively recently myself, so I know that passing is not just about putting in more hours. Often the difficult part is working out what you genuinely understand, what you are only recognising from notes, and how to apply the material when an exam question looks unfamiliar.

That is the kind of support I want the sessions to provide. Some students will want a detailed explanation of one chapter. Others will want to spend almost the whole session working through questions. Others may need help deciding what to revise next. I am happy to adapt the session accordingly.

My background

I work full time as an actuarial manager, with experience across pricing, asset-liability management and with-profits. I also hold a Master’s degree in Mathematics.

I passed every IFoA exam first time. In the three subjects I currently tutor, I scored CM1 91, CS1 88 and CB1 89. I do not want the tutoring to be about my own marks, but I think they are useful context for students choosing a tutor for these particular exams.

What sessions are like

Sessions are held online by video call with screen sharing. Beforehand, you can tell me which topics you want to cover, your preferred balance of theory and question practice, and anything specific you want me to look at. If you are not sure, I can help decide the best use of the session.

I am also happy to answer brief follow-up questions by email between sessions.
