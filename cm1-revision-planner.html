How it works

Set it up in a few minutes.

  1. Choose 4 to 16 weeks. The weekly focus changes automatically.
  2. Add your study hours. Enter hours completed so far and what you realistically have available each week.
  3. Follow the weekly focus. Use it as a guide, not a rigid timetable.
  4. Update confidence and mistakes occasionally. The workbook is deliberately light on admin.

Planner FAQs

Before you download.

Do I need to complete all 250 hours during my chosen revision window?

No. The IFoA figure is an overall recommended study time for CM1. Enter the hours you have already completed and treat the remaining figure as context rather than a rigid requirement.

What if the suggested weekly pace is unrealistic?

Use that as a warning that you may need to prioritise more aggressively. The planner is there to make the workload visible, not to tell you to force an impossible number of hours into a week.

Is the planner official IFoA material?

No. It is an independent planning tool. Always check the current IFoA syllabus and exam guidance for the sitting you are preparing for.
