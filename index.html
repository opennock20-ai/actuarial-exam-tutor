FAQs

Questions students often have.

Do I need regular weekly sessions?

No. You can book a one-off session for a particular topic or question, or arrange regular sessions during revision.

Can you help with a resit?

Yes. We can look at what did not work previously, identify weaker areas and build a more targeted approach for the next sitting.

Can I send questions before the session?

Yes. In fact, I encourage it. Sending topics or questions beforehand lets me prepare a more useful session for you.

Can I ask questions between sessions?

Yes. I am happy to answer brief follow-up questions by email between sessions.

Who do you tutor?

I welcome working actuarial students, university students, independent candidates, first-time sitters and resitters.
