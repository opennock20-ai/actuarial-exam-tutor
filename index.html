Make your revision time count

The challenge is not always learning more. It is knowing what to focus on.

IFoA subjects contain a lot of material. It is easy to spend hours rereading notes or practising comfortable areas without fixing what is actually costing you marks.

Know what to focus on

Identify weaker areas and prioritise the topics and skills most worth your remaining revision time.

Understand difficult topics

Work through concepts and questions step by step until you understand what you are doing and why.

Turn knowledge into marks

Improve question technique, time management and the way you apply what you know under exam conditions.

Plan the workload

Do not underestimate how much study time the subjects need.

The IFoA publishes recommended study hours for each subject. I found keeping an eye on those totals genuinely useful because it stopped me underestimating topics and leaving too much for the end.

Yes. One of the main aims of the tutoring is to make revision more focused. We can identify weaker areas and work out where your remaining time is most likely to make a difference.
