About me

Hi, I’m Oliver.

I’m a qualified actuary and actuarial manager offering 1-to-1 tutoring for CM1, CS1 and CB1.

Why I started tutoring

I completed the IFoA exams relatively recently, so I still remember what the process is actually like: a large syllabus, difficult topics and the constant question of whether you are using your revision time well.

One of the biggest challenges for me was not simply learning more material. It was knowing what I understood well enough, what genuinely needed more work and when it was time to stop reading notes and start doing questions.

That is the kind of support I want tutoring to provide. Sessions are not designed to replace your own study. They are there to make that study more effective, whether that means getting unstuck on a difficult topic, working through questions together or deciding where to focus next.

My actuarial background
