Focused revision

Do not spend the whole revision period rereading the course.

CB1 is broad, which makes passive revision tempting. But knowing the material and being able to produce a relevant answer under time pressure are different skills. We can identify weak areas, simplify difficult concepts and spend more time actively producing answers and applying the material.

Practical support: Written-answer technique can be a full session in its own right: interpreting the command word, structuring the response and generating enough relevant points without wasting time.

This can be useful if you are...

  • sitting CB1 for the first time
  • resitting and wanting a more focused plan
  • finding accounting or financial statements unfamiliar
  • knowing the notes but struggling with written answers
  • unsure what to prioritise across a broad syllabus
